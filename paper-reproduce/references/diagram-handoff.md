# Explanatory-diagram handoff

Use this reference only after classifying the target by **how it was produced and what evidence role it plays**. Visual form alone never decides ownership.

## Keep computed structures in Paper Reproduce

A target remains a Paper Reproduce result when its nodes, links, topology, hierarchy, or layout were produced by an executable scientific process whose outcome is being reproduced. This includes, for example:

- architectures found by neural architecture search;
- trees produced by clustering or inference;
- topologies selected by optimization;
- model structures that change as a reported result of training, pruning, or adaptation.

Such targets may look like schematics, but their structure is data. Reproduce the generating search, training, optimization, or algorithm and validate the resulting scientific claim.

## Hand off explanatory drawings

Hand off only author-drawn theory, mechanism, process, workflow, route, circuit, or conceptual diagrams whose scientific content is an explanation rather than the output of the computation under study. The appropriate objective is then faithful, editable reconstruction, not experimental recomputation.

When `sci-diagram-pptx` is already available, transfer that target there. If it is unavailable, explain the handoff and suggest an explicit user-managed installation; Paper Reproduce must not install or update another skill automatically. Do not create a Paper Reproduce execution result merely to redraw it.

For a mixed figure, classify panels separately when their boundaries and captions make them independent. A computed panel stays in Paper Reproduce; an explanatory panel may be handed off. Preserve the parent figure and panel identity in both routes.

## Transfer only necessary context

Pass the unchanged target image or traceable crop, paper locator, figure and panel label, complete caption, and only the nearby text needed to understand wording, formulas, grouping, and connector meaning. Also pass the user's requested format and editability constraints.

Do not transfer source-search history, runtime probes, Paper Reproduce validation records, manifests, or delivery machinery. Once the explanatory target is accepted by the receiving skill, Paper Reproduce no longer validates it.

Return a target to Paper Reproduce only when later evidence shows that the displayed structure was itself generated by a computation being reproduced. Resolution, authoring difficulty, or the mere presence of boxes and arrows is not a reason to change ownership.
